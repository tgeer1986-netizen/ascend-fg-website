Senior household income protection

Could one lost monthly check force a major change?

Many retired couples build their household budget around two Social Security checks. After one spouse dies, the surviving spouse may receive the higher applicable benefit—not both checks—so monthly household income can fall sharply.

Life insurance can create a separate pool of money the surviving spouse may use as a monthly allowance while adjusting financially.
