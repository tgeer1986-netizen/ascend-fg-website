Free household income-gap review

Could the surviving spouse keep the same home on one Social Security payment?

Many retired couples rely on two monthly Social Security payments. When one spouse dies, eligible retirement and survivor payments generally are not added together. The survivor may receive the higher applicable amount, potentially leaving a meaningful monthly household-income gap.
