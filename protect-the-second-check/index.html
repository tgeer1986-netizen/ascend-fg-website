A free retirement-income protection review

Could one surviving spouse keep the same home on one Social Security payment?

Many retired couples depend on two monthly Social Security payments. When one spouse dies, eligible retirement and survivor payments generally are not added together. The surviving spouse may receive the higher applicable amount, which can leave a meaningful monthly household-income gap.

The overlooked senior-planning question

Funeral costs are only one part of the risk.

A surviving spouse may still have the same mortgage or rent, utilities, groceries, insurance and property expenses—but less monthly household income. A life insurance death benefit can provide money the beneficiary may use to create a temporary monthly bridge, pay final expenses, reduce debt or buy time to make thoughtful decisions instead of immediate ones.
